About Alappuzha

Alappuzha, also known as Alleppey, is one of the most beautiful destinations in Kerala. It is especially known for its scenic backwaters, traditional houseboats, peaceful waterways and beautiful village landscapes. A visit to Alappuzha offers travellers a relaxing experience surrounded by water, greenery and Kerala's traditional lifestyle.
