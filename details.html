🎭 Kathakali

Kathakali is a traditional classical dance-drama of Kerala. It is famous for colourful costumes, makeup, music and expressive movements.

🛶 Chundan Vallam

Chundan Vallam is the traditional snake boat of Kerala. Vallam Kali is a famous traditional boat race.

⛰️ Munnar

Munnar is famous for green tea plantations, mountains, waterfalls and beautiful scenery.

🌴 Alleppey

Alleppey is famous for beautiful backwaters, houseboats and village life.

🏖️ Kovalam

Kovalam is famous for its beautiful beaches, sea views and lighthouse.

🌿 Wayanad

Wayanad is known for forests, waterfalls, mountains and beautiful nature.
