Popular Tourist Places

Explore some of the most beautiful destinations in Kerala.

🌿 Munnar

A beautiful hill station famous for tea plantations, misty mountains, waterfalls and cool weather.

🌳 Wayanad

A peaceful destination known for forests, waterfalls, wildlife, caves and scenic hills.

🚤 Alappuzha

Famous for Kerala backwaters, houseboats, village landscapes and relaxing cruises.

🏖️ Kovalam

A beautiful coastal destination known for beaches, sunsets and relaxing seaside experiences.

💧 Kerala Waterfalls

Enjoy beautiful waterfalls surrounded by lush greenery and peaceful natural scenery.

🎭 Kathakali

Experience Kerala's traditional performing art, colourful costumes and cultural heritage.

🌍 Explore More Destinations

Want to visit places beyond the regular Kerala destinations? Explore Vagamon, Gavi, Guruvayur, Thiruvananthapuram, Fort Kochi, Mattancherry, Ooty, Kodaikanal, Madurai and Rameshwaram.
