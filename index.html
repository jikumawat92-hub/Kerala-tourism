A Munnar Group Adventure Like No Other

You do not need to come with friends. Come alone, meet new people and become part of a group that shares laughter, music, stories, food and unforgettable moments.

🌿 More Than Just a Trip

Imagine travelling to the beautiful hills of Munnar with people you have never met before. At first, everyone may be a stranger. But somewhere between the first conversation, the first laugh and the first shared meal, strangers can become friends. This trip is about enjoying the journey together — not simply reaching the destination.

🗓️ Your 3-Day Experience

🌄 Day 1 — Meet, Explore & Celebrate

Arrive in Munnar and meet the other travellers. Get to know each other, explore beautiful surroundings, enjoy group activities and spend the evening together with music, games, stories and a campfire.

🌿 Day 2 — Explore, Cook & Create Memories

Explore the beauty of Munnar, visit scenic places, enjoy nature and take plenty of photographs. Later, come together as a group, prepare food, share the meal and enjoy another memorable evening.

❤️ Day 3 — One Last Adventure

Enjoy the final morning together, have breakfast, capture group photographs and spend some more time exploring Munnar before the journey comes to an end. You may leave Munnar, but the memories stay with you.
