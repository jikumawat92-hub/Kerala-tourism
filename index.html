Popular Destinations

🌄 Munnar

Munnar welcomes you with misty mountains, endless tea plantations and a cool refreshing breeze. It is the perfect place to escape from busy city life and create beautiful memories.

🚤 Alleppey

Enjoy the peaceful beauty of Kerala's backwaters. Relax on a traditional houseboat, watch the green landscapes and enjoy beautiful sunsets over calm waters.

🌿 Wayanad

Wayanad is a paradise of green mountains, forests and waterfalls. Nature lovers and adventure seekers can enjoy the peaceful surroundings and refreshing atmosphere.

🌊 Kovalam

Feel the sea breeze, listen to the waves and enjoy a beautiful sunset at Kovalam. Its beaches and Arabian Sea views make it a wonderful place to relax.
