About Munnar

Discover one of Kerala's most beautiful hill destinations.

Munnar is a famous hill station in Kerala, India, surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations, forests, waterfalls and peaceful valleys. It is a popular destination for couples, families, friends, honeymoon travellers and group tours.


A Munnar trip is perfect for sightseeing, nature experiences, photography, trekking, relaxing in the hills and enjoying the cool mountain atmosphere.
