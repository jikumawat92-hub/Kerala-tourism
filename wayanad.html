About Wayanad

Discover the natural beauty and peaceful landscapes of Wayanad.

Wayanad is a beautiful destination in Kerala, surrounded by green forests, mountains, waterfalls, wildlife and peaceful valleys. It is a popular destination for families, couples, friends, honeymoon travellers and groups.


A Wayanad trip is ideal for sightseeing, nature experiences, wildlife, trekking, photography and exploring the beautiful Western Ghats.
